# FocusLock Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 17, 2026

Welcome to FocusLock: App Blocker (the "App"). We respect your privacy and design the App to work locally on your device whenever possible. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, how it is stored, and what choices you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

FocusLock does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data. The current version does not use accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking, or external servers for user data.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide focus and blocking features, FocusLock may store the following information locally on your device:

- Screen Time authorization state;
- selected apps, websites, or categories;
- active focus timer state;
- recurring schedule rules;
- completed session counts, focused time, and streak information;
- selected alternate app icon.

This information is used only to provide App functionality and is not sent to our servers.

## 3. Screen Time and Family Controls

FocusLock uses Apple's Screen Time and Family Controls frameworks to let you choose apps, websites, and categories to shield during focus sessions. The permission flow is controlled by Apple. You can revoke Screen Time access at any time in iOS Settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The current version of FocusLock does not include advertising, analytics, account login, or payment processing services. If future versions add third-party services, we will update this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy information.

## 5. Children's Privacy

FocusLock does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not provide user-generated content, social networking features, advertising, or account-based tracking.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

You can delete local App data by deleting the App from your device. You can also change Screen Time permission and related system permissions in iOS Settings.

If you have privacy questions, please contact us using the email address below.

## 7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice through the App, App Store listing, or another appropriate channel.
