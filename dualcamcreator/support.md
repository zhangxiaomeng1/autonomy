# DuoCue Support

## Recording checklist

1. Grant camera and microphone access when entering the Studio.
2. Add or import a script in Scripts.
3. Choose the layout, orientation, quality, and teleprompter mode.
4. Tap Start Recording and keep enough free storage for the take.
5. Open Takes to review the result and export it to Photos.

## If front and rear capture is unavailable

Some iPhone models do not support the requested front/rear camera combination or quality at the same time. DuoCue falls back to one-camera teleprompter recording and explains the limitation in Studio.

## If voice follow is unavailable

Voice Follow requires device-side speech recognition support and permission. When it is unavailable or fails during a take, the teleprompter continues with Timed Scroll.
