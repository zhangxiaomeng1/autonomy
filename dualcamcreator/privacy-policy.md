# DuoCue Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-11

DuoCue is an on-device camera and teleprompter app. The app does not require an account and does not send videos, scripts, speech recognition results, or camera and microphone content to a server.

## Information processed on your device

- Camera and microphone data are used to preview and record a take.
- Speech recognition, when enabled and supported, is requested as on-device recognition and is used only to move the teleprompter.
- Scripts and recording metadata are stored locally in the app.
- Photos access is used only when you choose to export a finished video to the Photos library.

## Information we collect

DuoCue does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, contacts, precise location, or account information. The app has no analytics, advertising, cloud sync, or live streaming service.

## Your choices

You can deny camera, microphone, speech recognition, or Photos access. You can delete scripts and recordings from inside the app. You can also revoke permissions in iOS Settings.
