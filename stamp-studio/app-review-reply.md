# App Review 回复草稿（Stamp Studio 拒审 — 缺少 EULA 链接）

> 用法：复制下面「英文版」或「中文版」之一，粘贴到 App Store Connect 的 Resolution Center，或直接回复拒审邮件。
> 注意：回复只是说明，**真正触发复审需要在 App Store Connect 保存描述后点「Submit for Review」重新提交**。

## 英文版（推荐，App Review 处理最快）

Subject: Re: App Store Review — Missing EULA link for auto-renewable subscriptions

Hello,

Thank you for your message. We have updated the app metadata to include a functional Terms of Use (EULA) link, as required for apps offering auto-renewable subscriptions.

The App Description now links to the Apple Standard License Agreement (EULA):
https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

Our subscription terms (billing, renewal, cancellation, free trial, price changes, and taxes) are disclosed in the EULA referenced above. Please let us know if any further information is needed, and we appreciate your continued review.

Best regards,
Stamp Studio Team
