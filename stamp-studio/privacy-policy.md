# Stamp Studio Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 12, 2026

Stamp Studio (the "App") is a local-first PDF stamping and signature tool. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information when you use it.

## 1. Information We Collect

Stamp Studio does not collect, sell, rent, share, or transmit your personal information to our servers. The App has no user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking, or cloud-sync service in the current version.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

When you use the App, it stores the following information only on your device:

- imported document copies and their local metadata;
- signatures, stamps, and images you create or import;
- placement, size, rotation, and name settings for marks on your documents; and
- exported PDF files until you remove them from your device.

This information is used only to provide the App's document-editing features. You can remove locally stored App data by deleting documents and assets in the App or by deleting the App.

## 3. Camera and Photo Library

The App requests camera or photo-library access only after you choose to capture or import an image for stamp extraction. Selected images are processed locally on your device. The App does not upload those images to us.

## 4. Sharing and Exports

When you choose to export or share a PDF, iOS presents its standard share sheet. Any recipient, app, or service you select handles the shared file under its own privacy practices. Stamp Studio does not control those recipients or services.

## 5. Children's Privacy

Stamp Studio does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It has no social, advertising, or account features.

## 6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's data practices change. The current version will be posted at this URL.
