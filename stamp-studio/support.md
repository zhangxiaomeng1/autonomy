# Stamp Studio Support

Stamp Studio helps you create digital stamps and handwritten signatures, place them on PDFs, and export a new copy from your iPhone or iPad.

## Getting Started

1. Open **Files** and import a PDF.
2. Open the document and choose a signature or stamp from **Assets**.
3. Move, resize, or rotate the mark on the page.
4. Tap **Export** to share the finished PDF.
