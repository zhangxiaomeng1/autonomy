# RatePilot Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 21, 2026

Welcome to RatePilot: FX Pricing (the "App"). We respect your privacy and design the App to work locally on your device whenever possible. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, how it is stored, and what choices you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

RatePilot does not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data. The current version does not use accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party tracking, or external servers for user data.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide currency conversion and app-pricing estimate features, RatePilot may store the following information locally on your device:

- selected default currency;
- favorite currency codes;
- the latest downloaded exchange-rate response and update time; and
- prices entered for local calculations while you use the App.

Currency settings and cached exchange rates are used only to provide App functionality and are not sent to our servers. App-pricing suggestions are calculated locally. Prices entered for calculations are not uploaded by RatePilot.

## 3. Exchange-Rate Data Provider

When you open or manually refresh RatePilot, the App requests public exchange-rate data from ExchangeRate-API at `open.er-api.com`. RatePilot sends the requested base currency as part of the URL. The provider may receive standard network information, such as your IP address and request metadata, in order to deliver the response. Its handling of that information is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The current version of RatePilot does not include advertising, analytics, account login, or payment processing services. If future versions add third-party services, we will update this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy information.

## 5. Children's Privacy

RatePilot does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not provide user-generated content, social networking features, advertising, or account-based tracking.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

You can delete locally stored RatePilot data by deleting the App from your device. You may continue to use the latest cached rates when a live refresh is unavailable.

If you have privacy questions, please contact us using the email address below.

## 7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will provide notice through the App, App Store listing, or another appropriate channel.
